Course Contents & Lectures

Key Topics

  • Introduction to HCI and effective design in the interaction design process
  • Design principles: visibility, flow, affordance, metaphors, feedback, and consistency
  • Understanding users and data gathering through interviews, questionnaires, and observations
  • Requirements formulation, literature reviews, scenarios, and use cases
  • User interfaces and modalities
  • Design, prototyping, and construction using low- and high-fidelity prototypes
  • Evaluation: goals, methods, and case studies
  • Qualitative and quantitative data analysis
  • Group course project: prototype submission and presentation
  • Future directions in HCI and interaction design
